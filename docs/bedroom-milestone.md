# Layered bedroom milestone (local work, not published)

Based on main d6037a609fdc44a991518f88fae7eb8540803007. No account/backend changes, APK export, or publication.

## Play
Play as Guest → Enter local adventure → choose male/female base, appearance, class and local username → Begin local adventure → appear dressed beside the bed. Continue returns to the existing local guest slot. The touch joystick moves at analog speed; arrow keys/WASD and floor taps remain available. E/Space or the nearby interaction button opens the chest or mirror. Escape/Android Back closes a panel, then returns to title. Save & title explicitly saves.

## Implemented
- Original 1536×1024 painted empty room plus nine registered transparent layers: bed, chest, mirror, bedside table/lamp, dresser, wall pictures, rug, rear-facing chair and side-view bookshelf. Furniture has separate collision footprints and depth ordering. Floor bounds follow the painted trapezoid. Revision 5 places the bed against the left wall, chest at its foot, mirror far right, bookshelf against the right wall and rear-facing chair two floorboards lower; all interaction anchors are walkable and reachable from the wake spawn. Door is an interaction anchor only; no town is added.
- Immediate walkable bedside spawn, with no hidden-character or Wake up step. Avatar scale is 1.05 (previously 0.72), preserving the foot anchor, collision footprints, interaction radii and depth sorting. The touch joystick has a 16% deadzone, clamped analog magnitude, single-finger ownership, and release/cancel/focus-loss resets; other fingers can operate interface buttons. Modal panels reset movement and cannot activate the pad beneath them. The new renderer requires aligned body plus class-armor layers and never falls back to underwear. All 12 male/female class outfits are integrated. Each has four directional idle frames and eight distinct paired body/armor walking phases in each direction, authored at 16fps with a 60 logical px/s stride baseline. Player movement is now 50% faster at 90 logical px/s; distance-driven playback gives an effective 24fps at full speed, slows with analog input, and stops when blocked. Title chase timing and artwork are unchanged. The mechanically constructed gait remains somewhat stiff, especially at the knees and side boots; it is a test-build animation, not a polished final cycle.
- Single local guest save, atomic temporary-write/rename, no credential storage or fabricated online authentication. The new-character chest contains exactly five healing potions and one class-matched weapon. Class armor starts equipped. Weapons can be taken, equipped, unequipped and stored; class restrictions and persisted equipment prevent duplicate grants. Bag/chest transfer one item at a time, with bounded loaded counts and rollback on write failure. Invalid saves are retained for recovery and never silently overwritten.
- Creator recovered from surviving QA source. Initial creation allows male/female choice. Mirror exposes hair, hair color, eyes and skin; base/class/username controls are locked, and the save layer independently accepts only four cosmetic fields. Cross-base hair is rejected. Randomize stays within current base.
- Full cosmetic rendering remains pending approved layer/palette assets. The creator explicitly labels artwork unavailable instead of pretending a finished look. Selections persist; the creator and room display the actual dressed starter outfit, without falsely applying unimplemented hair/eye/skin recoloring. Equipped weapons persist in inventory; held weapon animation is not integrated.
