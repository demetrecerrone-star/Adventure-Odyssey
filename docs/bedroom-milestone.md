# Layered bedroom milestone (local work, not published)

Based on main d6037a609fdc44a991518f88fae7eb8540803007. No account/backend changes, APK export, or publication.

## Play
Play as Guest → Enter local adventure → choose male/female base, appearance, class and local username → Begin local adventure → Wake up. Continue returns to the existing local guest slot. Arrow keys/WASD or floor taps move; touch arrows are also available. E/Space or the nearby interaction button opens the chest or mirror. Escape/Android Back closes a panel, then returns to title. Save & title explicitly saves.

## Implemented
- Original 1536×1024 painted empty room plus nine registered transparent layers: bed, chest, mirror, bedside table/lamp, dresser, wall pictures, rug, rear-facing chair and side-view bookshelf. Furniture has separate collision footprints and depth ordering. Floor bounds follow the painted trapezoid. Revision 4 places the bed against the left wall, chest at its foot, mirror far right, bookshelf against the right wall and rear-facing chair two floorboards lower; all interaction anchors are walkable and reachable from the wake spawn. Door is an interaction anchor only; no town is added.
- Wake-up start at the bed, followed by a walkable bedside spawn. There is no finished sleeping animation. The new renderer requires aligned body plus class-armor layers and never falls back to underwear. All 12 male/female class outfits are integrated. Each has four directional idle frames and eight distinct paired body/armor walking phases in each direction, played at 16fps with 60 logical px/s movement. The mechanically constructed gait remains somewhat stiff, especially at the knees and side boots; it is a test-build animation, not a polished final cycle.
- Single local guest save, atomic temporary-write/rename, no credential storage or fabricated online authentication. The new-character chest contains exactly five healing potions and one class-matched weapon. Class armor starts equipped. Weapons can be taken, equipped, unequipped and stored; class restrictions and persisted equipment prevent duplicate grants. Bag/chest transfer one item at a time, with bounded loaded counts and rollback on write failure. Invalid saves are retained for recovery and never silently overwritten.
- Creator recovered from surviving QA source. Initial creation allows male/female choice. Mirror exposes hair, hair color, eyes and skin; base/class/username controls are locked, and the save layer independently accepts only four cosmetic fields. Cross-base hair is rejected. Randomize stays within current base.
- Full cosmetic rendering remains pending approved layer/palette assets. The creator explicitly labels artwork unavailable instead of pretending a finished look. Selections persist; the creator and room display the actual dressed starter outfit, without falsely applying unimplemented hair/eye/skin recoloring. Equipped weapons persist in inventory; held weapon animation is not integrated.
